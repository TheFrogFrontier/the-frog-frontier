Before you print one

Use PETG if the dish will hold standing water. Wood-fill PLA looks better but needs sealing, and PLA softens in a warm tank. Print the hull at 0.12 mm layers if you want the plank texture to show, and skip ironing, which flattens it out.

Why the blanks are blank

There is no humidity number printed anywhere in the handbook. That is deliberate, and it is the thing I most want you to understand before you buy.

A White’s tree frog, a Pacman and a dart frog want three different tanks. A care sheet that fills those numbers in for you is guessing about an animal it has never met. So every value in these pages is a line you write on: your species, your setup, your vet. That is also why one handbook works for any frog.

What the pages give you is the structure. The right questions, in the right order, in language a nervous twelve-year-old can follow at eight in the evening when the frog hasn’t eaten.

Questions

How do I get the files after paying?

A download link is emailed to you automatically as soon as the payment clears, and the link stays good if you need the file again later. Check the spam folder on the first one.

Can I use the handbook for a frog that isn’t a tree frog?

Yes. Because the care values are blanks you fill in, the same handbook works for a Pacman, a dart frog, a toad, or anything else you keep. If you keep more than one, print a second copy of the two pages that describe an individual animal.

Can I edit the PDFs?

They’re finished print-ready files rather than templates, so the text isn’t editable. The pages are meant to be filled in by hand, which is the point when a sitter needs to scribble a note at the tank.

What do I get with an STL?

The mesh file itself, plus a short notes page with the material, layer height and orientation I use. No slicer profile, because those don’t travel well between machines.

I’m a professional pet sitter. Can I hand these to clients?

The standard license covers printing copies for your own frog and your own sitter. For handing them out across a pet-sitting business, email me and I’ll sort out a commercial license.

Do you take refunds?

Digital files can’t really be returned once they’re delivered. But if something won’t open or prints wrong, email me and I’ll fix it or refund you.
