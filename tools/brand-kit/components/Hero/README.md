# Hero
The shop banner as a live layout: the frog on its disc at the left, the name on two lines in the center, leaves on a matching disc at the right.

- Classes: `ff-hero`, `ff-hero__side`, `ff-hero__side--leaves`, `ff-hero__name`, `ff-hero__est`.
- The two sides carry equal weight. Do not move the frog to the center or drop the right-hand disc on desktop.
- Below 640px it stacks: frog, then name, and the leaf side is hidden.
- The name is always "The Frog Frontier" in uppercase on two lines, breaking after "Frog".
- For places that need one flat image (Etsy, social), use `assets/Reference/shop-banner-1600x400.jpg`.
- You provide: nothing for the default. An optional line of body text and one Button may sit under the Est. line.
