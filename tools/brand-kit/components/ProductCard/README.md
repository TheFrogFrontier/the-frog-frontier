# ProductCard
A product tile: the item on a yellow disc with a pink disc overlapping, then name, a script note, price and a button.

- Classes: `ff-card`, `ff-card__art`, `ff-card__name`, `ff-card__note`, `ff-card__row`, `ff-card__price`.
- Product photos go in `ff-card__art` as an `<img>`. Cut-out photos on a transparent or white ground work best, because the image is blended onto the disc.
- Alternate which disc is larger from card to card so a grid does not look stamped out.
- The preview shows a leaf as a stand-in. Replace it with the real product photo.
- You provide: image, name, note, price and the link.
